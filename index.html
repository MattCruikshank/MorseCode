How to read the card

  • Every letter starts at the antenna. The first symbol picks a side: a dot goes right to E, a dash goes left to T.
  • On the right half, another dot keeps going right and a dash steps down. The left half is the mirror image: another dash keeps going left and a dot steps down.
  • A circle means the letter ends in a dot. A bar means it ends in a dash. They light green and red as you key.
  • A short press is a dot and a long press is a dash. Stop for a moment to finish the letter, and pause longer to add a space. Change the speed in Settings.
